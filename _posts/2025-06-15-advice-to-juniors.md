After two years in the Communication Engineering program at NEU, I have compiled some advice for junior students navigating this critical period of undergraduate study. The choices you make now — in coursework, research, and competitions — can significantly shape your future opportunities.

## Course Selection

The junior year is when the curriculum gets interesting. Key courses include:

- **Signals and Systems**: The mathematical foundation for everything that follows
- **Digital Signal Processing**: Practical applications of signal theory
- **Communication Principles**: Core concepts in modulation, coding, and transmission
- **Computer Networks**: Essential for understanding modern communication systems

## Research Preparation

If you are considering graduate school, start building research experience now:

- Approach professors whose work interests you — most are happy to mentor motivated undergraduates
- Participate in research-oriented competitions like mathematical modeling or the IC Competition
- Read papers regularly, even if you don't fully understand them at first

## Graduate School Applications

For those considering master's or Ph.D. programs, start preparing early. Identify target programs, build relationships with potential advisors, and ensure your academic record reflects your capabilities.
