As a new student at Northeastern University's Hunnan Campus, I found that many incoming freshmen face the same challenges: navigating the sprawling campus, understanding academic resources, and settling into dormitory life. This guide compiles everything I wish I had known on day one.

## Campus Overview

Hunnan Campus is the newest NEU campus, located in the Hunnan District of Shenyang. It houses most of the engineering and science programs, including the School of Computer Science and Engineering.

## Key Tips

- **Getting Around**: The campus is large — a bicycle is highly recommended. Campus shuttle buses run every 15 minutes during peak hours.
- **Dining**: The main canteen offers affordable meals (¥10-15 per meal). The second-floor food court has more variety.
- **Study Spaces**: The library gets crowded during exam season. The teaching buildings' self-study rooms are great alternatives.
- **Internet**: Campus WiFi covers all academic buildings. Dormitory internet requires a separate subscription.

## Academic Resources

NEU provides free access to CNKI, IEEE Xplore, and other academic databases through the campus network. Make use of these early — they're invaluable for coursework and research.
